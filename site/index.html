Tips and Resources

Practical Tips

  • Switch off lights and electrical devices when they are not in use.
  • Walk, cycle or use public transport for shorter journeys where possible.
  • Reduce food waste by planning meals and using leftovers.
  • Reuse shopping bags, bottles and containers instead of choosing disposable alternatives.
